# UNZA DigiHealth Mobile App – Senior Software Engineer Master Plan for Replit

Based on the uploaded proposal, the mobile application must digitize the entire UNZA clinic workflow while remaining student-centric and scalable. The app should cover Student, Doctor, Pharmacist, Laboratory, Mental Health, and HIV/AIDS service workflows as defined in the proposal.  

---

# 1. Product Vision

Create a unified mobile healthcare platform that:

* Reduces clinic queues
* Enables online consultations
* Digitizes prescriptions
* Digitizes laboratory requests/results
* Provides mental health support
* Provides HIV/AIDS support services
* Delivers real-time notifications
* Maintains complete patient history
* Improves coordination between healthcare personnel

This directly addresses the problems identified in the proposal.

# Final Replit Instruction

**Build UNZA DigiHealth as a production-grade multi-role mobile healthcare platform with six independent dashboards (Student, Doctor, Pharmacist, Laboratory, Mental Buddy, HIV/AIDS Centre), centralized medical records, consultation workflows, digital prescriptions, laboratory workflows, notification systems, role-based security, and healthcare-grade audit logging. The system must prioritize queue reduction, healthcare accessibility, confidentiality, and coordination among all healthcare units while remaining optimized for Android mobile devices.**
